Latest Research

LLM Research and Verification (November 2026)

A study of stance prompting and a separate record of the recalculation and verification of its results.

Preprint

What Do Stance Prompts Change in Large Language Models — A Placebo Controlled Investigation of Prompt Effects and Measurement Challenges

A first study comparing stance prompts with placebo and other controls. Response features changed, but no consistent task-performance improvement over placebo controls was found. The paper distinguishes response changes from internal states or subjective experience and examines limitations in evaluation and end-to-end reproducibility.

Preprint, not peer reviewed · v1 · Published November 18, 2026

Open record
Report

Verification Report for Study 1 of Stance Prompts in Large Language Models

A verification record using a separate implementation to recalculate results for 9,240 saved responses, confirming agreement in predefined pass/fail results and recovered logs. It documents analytical defects and unverified areas. This is not independent human peer review, and the public materials do not enable reproduction of the full workflow.

Verification report · v1 · Published November 15, 2026

Preprint

Temporal Dissociation of Ownership and Agency

Double posteriority in flow experience and a design for measuring it. Proposes separately estimating the time constants of the ownership and agency saturation curves. August 2026, 38 references.

Protocol

Temporal Dissociation of Ownership and Agency: A Single-Participant Feasibility Protocol Using Low-Burden Onset Measures

A single-participant protocol for collecting low-burden reports of bodily ownership and agency onset in the moving rubber hand illusion. It retains continuous ratings and time-constant estimation as a later stage. The protocol has not been carried out.

Document v2 is dated 4 September 2026; the initial OSF upload was 20 September. As checked on 8 November 2026, this project has no completed Registration.
